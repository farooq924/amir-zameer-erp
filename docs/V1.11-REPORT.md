# v1.11.0 Report - Documents (User Guide)

Date: 2026-11-28. Software developed by Brandistan | www.brandistan.pk | 0333-2532924

## Delivered
* New **Documents** menu item (`/documents`), last in the side menu, visible to every signed-in user (no new permission).
* Built-in user guide with 34 sections in reading order, from login and first-time setup to backups:
  Getting Started, Settings & Access, Customers & Orders, Fabric & Purchases, Staff & Payroll, Money & Accounts,
  Reports & WhatsApp, Offline & Sync, Security & Backup, Help (routine, common problems, updating the ERP).
* Guide text is in simple Roman Urdu on the owner's request (exception to the English-only rule); menu, headings and buttons stay English.
  On-screen button and field names are shown in bold exactly as they appear in the ERP.
* Each section: summary, numbered steps, every option explained, tips and warnings, and an **Open page** button
  (shows "No access for your role" when the user's role cannot open that page).
* Search across all sections, section list grouped by area (section picker on phones), Previous / Next.
* **Print section** and **Print full guide** (A4, with the Brandistan developer footer).
* Print fix for all in-app pages: long pages now flow over several printed pages instead of being cut at the screen height.
